How the calculation works Diesel cost = (total km ÷ mileage) × diesel price. EV cost = total km × kWh/km × tariff. If you enter a maintenance cost, it is added to the per-km cost. Total km / month = number of vehicles × km per day × days per month CO₂ is calculated at 2.68 kg/litre for diesel and 0.67 kg/kWh for grid electricity — approximate values, for comparison only. The vehicle purchase price is not included in this calculation.
